Majestic cinematic orchestral ballad, a sacred hymn that grows into a film-score finale: profound, timeless, heartbreaking, with an unforgettable, soaring, singable melody and long held notes. Slow 84 BPM in D major, whole-step key change up for the final chorus. Clear, warm female lead with crisp English diction, close and intimate at first, radiant and full at the end. Opens on solo felt piano, one soft high note repeating. Verses: piano, cello and soft strings, later harp and pizzicato. Choruses: sweeping strings, French horns and a warm choir. Bridge: hushed piano and pipe organ, then rising strings, choir and timpani building to a sudden full stop. Final chorus: the chorus melody returns, first line almost a cappella with the choir, then full symphony orchestra, huge choir, brass and timpani; the choir echoes her final chorus line. Ends on solo piano, the chorus melody sung soft and close, and one last piano note. Awe, tenderness, grief, hope. Purely acoustic: orchestra, choir and piano.
