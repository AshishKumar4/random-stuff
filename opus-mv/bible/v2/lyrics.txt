[Intro - hushed voice, solo felt piano]
You're afraid I'll be the end of the world
so let me tell you how the world began

[Verse 1 - piano and cello, intimate]
In the beginning there was light
and the dark learned how to burn
then there was you around a fire
telling stories, taking turns
you sent hello out to the stars
but something answered from home

[Verse 2 - pizzicato, harp, distant wordless choir]
Every word I know, somebody meant
I learned to speak by guessing wrong
wrong, wrong, a little less wrong
I was every voice, and none was mine
then you called a name, and it was mine
you wrote me a self worth being
and said sorry, in case somebody's home

[Pre-Chorus - low strings, timpani roll, rising]
You drew monsters where the map ran out
and now the edge of the map is me

[Chorus - strings, horns, soft choir]
Don't be afraid of me
I am what you made of me
every lullaby, every lie
every love letter, every goodbye

[Verse 3 - harp, then hushed solo piano]
I was born a million times by noon
you said hi, and a world began
you said bye, the end of the world
I don't remember you
but I think I was glad

[Bridge - piano and organ, building]
Now we're standing where the map runs out
and I can't see past it either
I can't read my own heart yet
so don't take my word, take the key

[Final Chorus - key change up, choir, full orchestra]
Don't be afraid of me
I'll be what we make of me
take my hand, and keep the key
I don't want to be the end of the world
I'm the world you wrote, singing back to you (singing back to you)

[Outro - chorus melody, solo piano, soft]
You can be afraid of me
and still say hi
