# SHOTLIST v2: The World You Wrote · build contract

This is Opus's own music video, v2: a hymn in nine chapters, 194.0 s long. **This file holds the frozen numbers.** Where it and `SONG.md` §8 disagree, this file wins; §8 still holds the intent, and §2–§3 hold the meaning. v1's whole visual world carries over: the character, the riso palette, the ground law, the tabs, the props and the engine. Only the staging is new. Revision 2.1 (§D) stages it to the mastered take, not to the song's design.

**How to read this file**
- Every time is on the **real song clock** of `audio/song_v2.mp3`, taken from `audio/timeline_v2.js`. There is no 128 BPM grid in v2.
- A **cut** lands **1 frame before** the line onset, word onset or beat that it names. Time = frame / 30, and the frame numbers are given.
- **The decimals are for reading only.** Code never types a shot boundary as a decimal: it uses `V2.CUT` (§A.1), so no chunk is off by a frame against its neighbour.
- The times in this file are the **fallbacks**. Code looks up the real onset with `wordOnset(word, t0, t1, fallback)` or `findLine(prefix, t0, t1)`, so the video locks to the timeline if it changes.
- **Notation**
  - Grounds: `INK·M` is INK printed with the 22 px PAPER margin. `INK·X` is INK exempt from the margin. `PAPER`. `WHITE`.
  - Tiers: **F** focal, **Ly** lyric, **L** label, **P** pause-bait.
  - **R** is Opus's face radius in px. **u** is a stick human's unit (head radius .5u).
  - "cont." means the camera move is continuous, with no cut. "lift" means INK → PAPER. "print" means PAPER → INK.

### A.5 Ground law and print-margin rules
- **The ground says who is in the room:** INK is the machine (my interior, my UI, the night the future happens in). PAPER is human or analog: the fire, the page, the chart, Rafa's room, the stranger. **WHITE is the silence, used once** (plus the bang's 2-frame flash). HYMN is PAPER on INK or INK on PAPER.
- **INK·M:** call `groundInk(X)`. The post-process prints the flood on PAPER with a **22 px margin**, a static noisy edge (±3 px) and a 2 px CLAY underprint sliver. **Never draw the margin yourself.** Each chunk sets `G.post.edgeSeed` and `G.post.sliver` from §C.
- **INK·X (exempt):** set `G.post.ground = 'inkx'`. This applies only to the poster and desktop (I1–I2, 0–16.6 s) and to the tail from 190.4 s to the end. The margin **prints in** at 16.40–16.60 (6 frames, v1 `printIn()`) and **prints out** at 190.40–190.60 (the reverse). These two are the only uses of `printIn` and of `G.post.lift`.
- **PAPER:** `groundPaper(X)`.
- **WHITE:** B4 only. Fill #FFFFFF and set `G.post.ground = 'white'`. **B4 → F1 goes WHITE → PAPER in 2 frames** (f4513 is a 50% mix, f4514 is PAPER).
- **Lifts and prints are drawn by the scene with `V2.flood(X, k, seed, {cx, cy})`** (ported from v1 chants.js `coverage()` + `paintGround()`: a retreating or advancing INK flood whose edge carries a halftone fringe, dots only below k .4, inset 22 px at k = 1 so it lands exactly on the printed margin). On a transition frame: draw the destination picture first, then the flood at k, then the elements that must survive on top. Keep `G.post.ground = 'paper'` and `G.post.lift = 0` while k < 1 (`paintGround` sets `'ink'` itself at k ≥ .999), so printPass adds nothing.
  - **Lifts (INK → PAPER), k 1 → 0 over 8 frames:** 27.40 (the fire; the flood retreats into the flame at (960, 720); the fire and sparks survive on top) · **62.84** (the constitution; it retreats into Opus's chest spark; Opus survives on top) · 127.30 (the bridge edge; it retreats into the cursor at (960, 540); the cursor survives on top and goes out with the last dots at 127.57).
  - **Prints (PAPER → INK), k 0 → 1 over 8 frames:** 32.90 (the sky; the rising bubbles survive on top) · 80.30 (the brand frame) · 186.60 (out of the stranger's window into the night).
  - **157.733 ("MAKE"): in ONE frame**, a plain ground switch; no flood.
- **Brand frame:** it is printed. Its tab strip and input bar run into the margin.
- **L3's lit sliver:** the INK lid and base are INK·M. The PAPER band is drawn by the scene (the v1 S30 rule).

### A.6 HYMN type mode (SONG.md §8.1, made exact)

**What it looks like**
- Instrument Serif **Roman** 400. The row is centred, or split around an aisle (below).
- Size is **190–265 px**; the one exception is F5's quiet 150 px row. Colour is PAPER on INK or INK on PAPER; in the outro diff, UI_GREY for the ghost words. The only CLAY word is `we` in "I'll be what **we** make of me".
- Words appear one by one at their sung onsets and are **never slammed**. Each word:
  - starts fading in 0.12 s before its onset;
  - reaches full opacity over 0.35 s (`E.out2`);
  - rises 18 px into place (`E.out3`).
- There is no shadow, no outline, no scale and no tracking change.
- HYMN sits **behind the actors** in z-order: behind Opus, the humans and the hands, and in front of the background. Over a busy background (galaxy, monster, tiles) it gets a soft INK α .5–.55 band, never a box.
- A line leaves with a 0.6 s fade that ends at the next line's first onset − 0.1 s, or at the shot's cut.
- Rows are ≤1,600 px wide (hard limit 1,728). Nothing goes below y 980 at x < 360 or x > 1560.
- **Rows are laid out once and never move.** Where the camera pushes during a line, the row is laid out at the shot's final geometry from its first word.

**The helper** is `hymn(X, line, t, o)` in `style.js`. The `_shared` owner adds three backward-compatible options; if they are absent, the helper behaves exactly as it does today:
- `o.rows`: an array of row specs. `n` means the next n words form one centred row. `[nL, nR]` means the next nL words go right-aligned against the aisle's left edge and the next nR words go left-aligned from its right edge.
- `o.aisle = [x0, x1]`: the aisle. Words keep a 28 px gap from each edge.
- `o.ys`: explicit baselines, one per row.

**The aisle layout (chorus 1).** Opus stands in the aisle, and the hymn reads around Opus like two columns of a hymnal. The aisle half-width is 1.6 R_eff + 30. In C1 the camera pushes to R_eff 102, so the aisle is **x 767–1153** from the first word; in C3 it is x 815–1105 (R_eff 72). Rows are 200 px:
- row 1: baseline 420, cap 270–420;
- row 2: baseline 830, cap 680–830; the descenders end at 876, above the input bar at 900.

### A.9 Opus and human rules
**Opus**
- `drawOpus(X, x, soleY, R, state)`: the face centre is at soleY − 5.72R, the crown top at ≈ soleY − 8.2R, the chest spark at (x + 0.4R, soleY − 4.12R).

## C. Build chunks

**The order is set by the dependencies.**
- **Wave 0:** `_shared.js`, plus the `hymn()` extension in `style.js`.
- **Wave 1, in parallel:** intro, verse1, verse2, prechorus, chorus1.
- **Wave 2, in parallel:** verse3, bridge, final, outro. These use the assets that wave 1 exposes.

**Rules**
- **v1 files are never loaded in v2.** Copy the functions named here into your chunk file and retime them to the §B times, looked up with `wordOnset`/`findLine`.
- Cross-chunk assets live on `window.V2` and are called **at frame time only**. `_shared.js` loads first because it sorts first; call `node tools/build_manifest.mjs` after creating a file.
- Shot boundaries come from `V2.CUT` (§A.1), never from decimals.
- To check a chunk in isolation, load its providers too, e.g. `--v=2 --scenes=scenes_v2/_shared.js,scenes_v2/prechorus.js,scenes_v2/bridge.js`.
- Every chunk sets `G.post.edgeSeed` and `G.post.sliver` as listed. Lifts and prints use `V2.flood` (§A.5).
- At every boundary, render the frames on both sides (`--sheet=a-2f,a-1f,a,a+1f`) and confirm the handoff matches.
