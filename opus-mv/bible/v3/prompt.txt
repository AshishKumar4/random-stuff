Melancholic, epic melodic EDM-pop, a festival anthem with a catchy, unforgettable melody. 128 BPM, A minor. Clear, bright female lead vocal with crisp English diction, upfront in the mix. Bright, bittersweet supersaw lead drops replay the vocal hook with pitched vocal chops over a four-on-the-floor kick, sidechained chords and deep sub bass. Opens with solo grand piano playing the chorus hook melody, then her voice sings it. Verses: warm synth plucks and claps, building. Snare rolls lift the pre-choruses into soaring choruses with sung backing-vocal replies. After the first chorus, a drop: the lead replays the chorus under a shouted K-pop gang-vocal chant. The second chorus flows into a half-time piano bridge; drums build into the final chorus and the biggest drop, chant again. Ends hushed. Polished, radio-ready mix. Bittersweet, euphoric, playful, heartbreaking.
