Anthemic, bittersweet melodic EDM-pop with festival energy and K-pop gloss: an instantly catchy, unforgettable, singalong hook. Driving 128 BPM four-on-the-floor, A minor. Bright, clear, emotional female lead vocal with crisp English diction, upfront, keeping the original vocal melody exactly. Piano opening under the hook, then punchy plucks and claps; rising snare rolls into big, soaring choruses with stacked harmonies and sung backing-vocal replies. Explosive drops: a bright, bittersweet supersaw lead replays the chorus melody with pitched vocal chops over a pounding kick, sidechained chords and deep sub bass, with a shouted gang-vocal chant. Half-time piano bridge, then a huge build into the final chorus and the biggest drop. Polished, radio-ready, euphoric and heartbreaking at once.
