# SHOTLIST: End of the World (A Million Times a Day) · v4 build contract

The frozen numbers for every shot, taken from `BIBLE.md` v4 §5.1, §6, §7 and §8. **If this file and the bible disagree on a number, this file wins, and the bible gets fixed.** The bible keeps the staging, intent and beat-by-beat cue order; `cues.json` is generated from this file plus that order (BIBLE §8.12, which also lists the lint rules). Times are targets on the 128 BPM grid (1 beat = 0.46875 s; bar *n* starts at (n−1) × 1.875 s) and are remapped onto the chosen take.

**Notation.** bbox = x0–x1 × y0–y1 in px at 1920×1080. **Ground:** `INK·M` = INK printed with the paper margin; `INK·X` = INK, exempt from the margin (F0–S02, S43 only); `PAPER`; `WHITE`. **Tier:** **F** = FOCAL, **Ly** = lyric, **L** = LABEL, **P** = pause-bait. **R** = Opus's face radius in px. "cond" = Archivo HERO wght 900 at 75% width, "xcond" = 62.5%, "exp" = 125%. Mono = JetBrains Mono (0.6 em advance). Every symbol in ▮ ▶ ✻ ⏺ ⇥ ✓ ✕ → ■ ⏸ ⏭ ⏎ 👍 👎 ✅ 👁 🎲 goes through `drawRich`.
